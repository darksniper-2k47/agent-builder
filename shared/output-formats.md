# output-formats.md — how deliverables should look

## Briefs
Use `projects\templates\brief.template.md`. Sections: Real job · Knowledge · Skill/tool · Output ·
First step · Open questions. Validate with `tools\check_brief.py`.

## Plans and explanations for Denzel
Deliver as a clean, self-contained **HTML page** opened in a browser — not a wall of markdown.
(This is a standing rule, see the hub `CLAUDE.md`.) Inline CSS, works offline, his brand palette
(obsidian + crimson + gold). Then give the full `D:\...` path and `start "" "path"` it open.

## Copy (any lane)
Anti-hype. Third-grade reading level. Sentence case. No exclamation marks. Banned: synergy,
holistic, paradigm shift, leverage, unlock potential.

## Scripts / record-lists (content lanes)
- A shot/record list: exactly what Denzel points the camera at, in order.
- A script: spoken lines + on-screen notes, timed if it matters. Respect TikTok/Shorts safe zones
  (keep graphics out of the right 20%). Never put graphics on a speaker's face.
